About me

Learning to build software that helps people

I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Engineering at BLDEA's Dr. P. G. Halakatti College of Engineering and Technology, Vijayapura. I'm in my second year, and software development is the direction I want my career to take.

My programming journey started with C, where I learned how logic, memory and control flow fit together. I then moved to Python and Java, and I now practise data structures and algorithms to get better at solving problems step by step. Alongside that, I build web pages with HTML and CSS and turn what I learn into projects like my Blood Donation and Hospital Support System.

I learn fastest by building, so I keep adding small projects, reading code and fixing my own mistakes. I'm looking for an internship where I can contribute, get feedback from experienced developers and grow into a dependable engineer.
